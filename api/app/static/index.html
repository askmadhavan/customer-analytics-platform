What is this?

Shops lose money when customers quietly stop buying. This is called churn. Spotting those customers early gives you a chance to win them back.

The prediction comes from a machine learning model trained on 99,441 real orders from Olist, an online marketplace in Brazil. That is why amounts are in Brazilian reais (R$).

It is a demo, so the results are a guide, not a guarantee. Nothing you enter here is stored.
